Why the architecture matters

Intelligence is not permission.
Execution is not success.

Models do not own the source of truth. They don't get to grant themselves authority, rewrite failed history, or call a timeout a completed job.

Below are two small, executable proofs of the control boundaries that make long-running agent systems auditable. No inflated production metrics. No decorative frameworks.
