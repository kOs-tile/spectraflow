## Rules
- Aim **80% deep green/black + 15% quiet text/paper + 5% active accent** as a composition *heuristic*, not an inflexible accounting rule.
- The contrast break in the research chapter is purposeful: a single pale-green physical publication, not another pastel page template.
- Do not use bright neon lime, cyber HUD screens, glassmorphic SaaS cards or noisy gradients to signal AI.
- Preserve visible differentiation between **PASS**, **BLOCKED**, and **UNVERIFIED** with textual labels; never only color. Where the semantic distinction matters, not every state should be green.
- Preserve accessible focus rings, button states, and muted metadata text contrast, especially on mobile; adjust the palette locally if needed.
- The smoke-glass evidence layers may vary in deep green hue, but all provenance and receipt text must remain legible.
- Nothing about this palette is automatically applied to Pulse, dance-course clients, or other companies. Only **KAVI-branded** assets are candidates; client identity governs their design.
- This palette is a **candidate**, not a finalized universal brand specification until the user approves the rendered outcome. No production overwrite without approval.

## Integration
In this repository the theme is isolated as \`portfolio/v3/kavi-black-forest.css\`; it imports after \`style.css\`. Original V3 composition/motion scripts are untouched, allowing an exact comparison of art direction.

Theme quality gates: desktop/mobile screenshot contact sheets, 360px and 390px responsive layouts, reduced motion, keyboard interaction, no overflow and simulated model/host authority semantics unchanged.
